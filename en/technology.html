The staff dashboard groups records by activity. The six metrics from reaction through sequence each require at least eight samples under their own counting rules. Device and app setup

Follow a finger press from the grip to the staff dashboard.

  1. Haptic grips

    Each grip has four independent levers. A light shows which lever to press, and the grip vibrates on a full press.

    8 levers · signal about every 0.1 s
  2. Bluetooth

    Tablet app

    Guides training on an Android tablet and records each trial. If the internet drops, it keeps the records and sends them once reconnected.

    Sound · large text · practice first
  3. Internet

    Records server

    Stores trials by ID number, totals them by session and week, and calculates level suggestions.

    ID numbers, not names
  4. Web browser

    Admin web

    Shows center status and each participant's records, provides PDF reports and CSV, applies level suggestions and manages accounts.

    Staff apply suggestions

How records are stored

Follow a lever signal from calibration to a participant’s record and a suggested level.

  1. Grip signal

    The lever position is sent for each finger about every 0.1 seconds.

  2. Calibration

    Using the "released to fully pressed" length that staff calibrated, it is read as press depth (%).

  3. Trial record

    For each trial, the app saves the reaction time, result and press depth for each finger, along with any other levers pressed.

  4. Totals

    Records are gathered by session and week and shown in the admin web, PDF reports and CSV.

  5. Suggestion

    Levels are suggested from the records, and staff confirm before applying.

How values are calculated

These are the formulas the app uses for its records.

Accuracy
Sum of (success 1 · partial success 0.5 · failure 0) ÷ trials attempted × 100. Timeouts are left out of the denominator and counted separately.
Time taken
The median reaction time (ms) of trials, excluding timeouts and Rhythm play. Rhythm play records beat timing separately.
Completed share per hand
For each hand, times completed ÷ times attempted × 100. Left and right are shown separately and are not combined into one number.
Hold time
In Hand exercise, the sum of time actually held (counted only up to the target time) ÷ the sum of target times × 100.
Presses on the beat
(exact + good × 0.5) ÷ rhythm trials × 100. A missed note is recorded as a failure.
Press depth
The deepest point the target lever reached during a trial, as a percentage of its calibrated travel (0–100%). This describes lever movement. Compare readings only across trials for the same participant.
Other fingers pressed
Which other levers were fully pressed instead of, or along with, the target lever, and how often. Each new full press counts once.

The app records press depth and other fingers pressed separately. Neither is used to calculate accuracy or suggest a level. The definitions are drafts under development and may change.

How levels are suggested

It does not look at accuracy alone; it also looks at timeouts caused by not pressing.

  • Starting level

    Each activity starts at its easiest step. Staff set its difficulty and speed.

  • Trials that are counted

    Only trials at the current level in the last 28 days, and only after the level was last changed.

  • How timeouts affect suggestions

    If the timeout share is 20% or more, the level is not raised even when accuracy is high, and "Continue" is shown with the reason. At 50% or more, "Please check the position and the device once" is added. This keeps timeouts caused by not pressing from being read as "too hard" and lowering the level.

  • No immediate reversal

    After a level change, the system waits two sessions before suggesting another change.

  • Staff confirm level changes

    Staff decide in the admin web with [Apply], [Later] or [Ignore]. Applying, ignoring and changing, with the reasons, are kept in the history.

The threshold values are provisional and will be set after reviewing actual use records.

Planned activity suggestions

We are exploring a feature that uses AI to suggest training plans from accumulated training records. It is not part of the product yet, and its timing has not been set.

  1. Records

    Levels and session records build up for each activity.

  2. Suggestion

    Those records are used to suggest the next training plan.

  3. Staff confirmation

    Staff decide whether to apply it. The app does not change anything on its own.

Checking the applicable regulations comes first. It would only suggest a training plan; it would not judge a person's condition and is not a diagnosis or a decision about treatment.
