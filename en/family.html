In development

Plans for the next grip

We plan to develop a next-generation grip (v3) with a motion sensor (such as a gyro) that reads the orientation (tilt and rotation), position and movement path of the hand together with finger input.

All of this is in development, and the schedule and package may change. We make no claims of efficacy or therapeutic effect. Because home use differs from use at centers, we will confirm labeling and safety requirements separately before deciding on its launch and setup.
