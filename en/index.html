Product

A new kind of training experience, one fingertip at a time

Rehaptic is a digital training device that links a hand-operated grip with app content, so people can experience hand movement and memory and rhythm tasks together.

  • Each finger presses on its own

    Each of the four levers on a hand presses on its own, and vibration tells you which finger.

  • Three activities guided by the app

    The tablet app guides hand exercise, rhythm play and Follow the Numbers with voice and large text.

  • Records that build up automatically

    Participation records gather in the admin web and appear as per-activity level trends and PDF reports. AI personalization based on these records is in planning.

More on the technology

Roadmap

From fingers to hand movement, and on to AI analysis

  1. Now

    Finger input and records

    Eight independent levers, a tablet app and an admin web record performance by finger and suggest levels.

  2. Next generation · v3

    Hand movement too

    A grip with a motion sensor reads the tilt, rotation, position and path of the hand, and adds hand-movement contents.

  3. Planned

    AI analysis and personalization

    Finger and movement records are analyzed together to summarize trends and suggest plans. Staff confirm before applying.

Next generation · v3

From fingers to the movement of the hand

The next-generation grip (v3) carries a motion sensor (such as a gyro) and reads the orientation (tilt and rotation), position and movement path of the hand together with finger input. It leads to new contents and AI analysis.

  • A sensor that reads orientation and position

    A motion sensor inside the grip reads the tilt and rotation of the hand, and where it moved from and to (position and path).

  • Hand-movement contents

    We are building new contents in which you move your hand while holding the grip. Finger input and hand movement are used together.

  • AI analysis of measured data

    Finger records and movement records are gathered together to summarize trends and suggest plans. Staff confirm before applying.

The next generation (v3) is a development plan, and its items and schedule may change. Measured values are records of how training was done, not a diagnosis or a judgment of effect. The pictures and video above are concept illustrations, not actual product or app screens.
