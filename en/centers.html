Guided hand exercise for centers

For staff running programs in community centers, senior centers, day or overnight care centers and residential care facilities.

Planned launch: first half of 2027

Use a pair of grips with an Android tablet. Staff can review training records in a browser.

Watch a center session

How it runs

Guide a session from one tablet

The app gives the cues while a staff member guides the session.

Read the session steps

  1. Choose a participant

    Select a participant on the tablet to bring up today’s plan and the level for each activity. A typical example is three activities lasting about 14 minutes in total.

  2. Check the device

    Press each of the eight levers in turn. Check that the matching finger and number light up on the tablet.

  3. Explanation and practice

    Participants hear the instructions and try a practice round before they start.

  4. Activities and results

    There is a short rest between activities; at the end, the app shows what was done today and saves the records.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What do I need?

You need a left and right grip, an Android tablet paired with the grips, and a web browser for the staff dashboard.

If the internet drops for a moment, the tablet stores the records and sends them automatically once it is connected again.

Is the older adult's name saved in the records?

Participants are registered not by name but by an ID number set by the center (for example, A-01). Only the number is stored on the tablet and the server. We provide a spreadsheet template so that the table matching numbers to older adults is kept on just one of the center's office PCs.

Can a participant use one hand or leave out a finger?

For each older adult you can set "uses one hand" and "fingers to leave out of training". The setting applies only to that person and does not carry over to the next.

What if it is too hard or too easy?

At first, each activity starts at its easiest step. After that, staff can move the level and speed of each activity up or down one step at a time. The admin web tells you who might be ready for a higher or lower level based on performance records; staff decide.

What if sound or vibration is uncomfortable?

In the staff settings you can lower or turn off the sound volume, the voice guidance and the vibration on pressing.

What if an older adult presses the screen by mistake?

During training the only on-screen button an older adult can press is "I need help". Staff end the session and change settings.

What happens to the records of someone who withdraws consent?

A center administrator can delete all of that person's records in the admin web. Deleted records cannot be restored. If you need help, contact DAMUN.

Is Rehaptic a medical device?

Rehaptic is a finger-exercise training product. It has not been approved or certified as a medical device and is not intended to diagnose, treat or prevent disease. The values in the admin web are records of how training was done; medical decisions belong to a medical professional.
