DAMUN listens to its customers and to the people on the front line, and turns what people need into digital health experiences.

It starts with listening to many voices

DAMUN comes from the characters 多 (many) and 聞 (to hear). We listen to our customers, their families, the staff on site and our partners.

We bring the needs we find there into our products and services, and build digital health experiences that are easy to understand in daily life and can be used over time.

Starting with centers, then homes and overseas

We widen the circle one place at a time, to where hand exercise is needed.

  • Centers Planned launch: first half of 2027

    Rehaptic for welfare centers, senior centers, day and night care centers and care facilities. We are looking for centers to consider adoption with us. For centers

  • Home Planned launch: 2028 or later

    A mobile app for older adults and families to use at home, and a new grip with a gyro sensor for games and finger grip-strength exercise, are in preparation. For home

  • Overseas Exploring

    We are looking at Singapore first and working out how to expand across Southeast Asia.

Schedules are plans and may change. Everything shown is a plan in preparation, and we make no claims of efficacy or therapeutic effect.
