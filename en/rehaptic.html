A new training experience, one fingertip at a time

Rehaptic is a digital training device that links a hand-operated grip with app content and records finger-by-finger training under seven areas: reaction, separation, control, sustain, beat, sequence and coordination.

Grip

One lever for each finger

  • Swap a lever to change resistance

    Each lever has its own spring resistance. Pull out the whole lever and fit one with the resistance you want.

  • Press depth for each finger

    The app’s grip check shows the sensor distance in millimeters and the calibrated press depth as a percentage for each finger.

  • Feedback on a full press

    Lights, sound and the screen show which finger to use. The grip vibrates when the lever is fully pressed.

The independent finger input and haptic structure is the subject of three domestic patent applications (pending, not yet granted).

Activities

Training activities

Explore each activity to see the app in action and how to use the grips.

  • Hand exercise

    Press the lever that matches the lit number and hold it for the time shown. Sustain · Separation · Coordination

  • Rhythm play

    Press the matching lever as each note reaches its ring. Beat · Coordination · Sustain

  • Follow the Numbers

    Watch the numbers, then press the matching levers in the same order. Sequence · Separation

  • Mole catching

    When a mole pops up, press the matching lever to catch it. Reaction · Separation

  • Fist Dodge

    Squeeze the whole left hand to steer the hot-air balloon left and the whole right hand to steer it right, dodging the dark clouds. Coordination · Sustain

  • One Finger at a Time

    Gently press the lit lever while keeping your other fingers at rest. Separation · Control

  • Water Pour

    Press the lever to raise the water level. Hold it steady when it reaches the yellow band. Control · Sustain

  • Beat Echo

    Listen to the whole rhythm, then tap it back on your turn. Beat

Activities in development

Explore four game concepts for the next-generation grips. Watch how each game responds as you squeeze and move your arms.

Rhythm Touch

20 sec

Move your arm as a block approaches. Squeeze to light the blade on that side. Open your hand to switch it off.

Constellation Trail

16 sec

Take turns with your left and right hands. Squeeze at one star, trace a line to the next, then release.

Cloud Flight

16 sec

Tilt both grips to steer the paper plane. The gate stays closed with one grip held. Squeeze both together to open it.

Aurora Duet

16 sec

Raise and lower each hand to change the pitch of its note. The ribbons join and the chord sounds only while both grips are held.

These four films illustrate concepts in development. Activities and launch plans may change. What each area means and which values are recorded are on the technology page. We make no claims of efficacy or therapeutic effect.
