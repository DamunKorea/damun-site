A new training experience,
one fingertip at a time

Rehaptic is a digital training device that links a hand-operated grip with app content and records finger-by-finger training under seven areas: reaction, separation, control, sustain, beat, sequence and coordination.

01Grip

A haptic grip where each finger presses on its own

Following guidance from the screen, sound and light, users move their fingers and take part in tasks that involve remembering or reacting to a beat. We aim to combine a variety of activities with performance records so that repeated exercise stays engaging.

  • Independent levers and vibration

    Each of the four levers on a hand presses on its own. The screen and the light on the grip show which finger to press, and a vibration confirms a full press.

  • Sight, hearing and touch together

    The screen, sound and the light on the grip carry the same signal, and a vibration confirms each press.

  • A left-and-right pair

    A pair of left and right grips connects to the app as numbers 1 to 8.

The independent finger input and haptic structure is the subject of three domestic patent applications (pending, not yet granted).

02Contents

All contents

See Rehaptic's eight training contents, together with the areas each one covers.

  • Hand exercise

    Press the finger of the lit number and keep holding for a set time. Sustain · Separation · Coordination

  • Rhythm play

    Press falling circles in time with the background music. Beat · Coordination · Sustain

  • Follow the Numbers

    Watch numbers, remember them and press them in the same order. Sequence · Separation

  • Mole catching

    A reaction game in which you press the lit finger to catch the mole that pops up. Reaction · Separation

  • Fist Dodge

    Squeeze the whole left hand to steer the hot-air balloon left and the whole right hand to steer it right, dodging the dark clouds. Coordination · Sustain

  • One Finger at a Time

    Gently press only the lit finger and let the other fingers rest. Separation · Control

  • Water Pour

    Pour water as deep as you press the lever, match the yellow band and hold it there. Control · Sustain

  • Beat Echo

    Listen to the beat to the end, then press the same beat on your turn. Beat

In development

Contents to be added later. The lineup and schedule may change.

  • Seasonal and regional theme packs

    Content bundles themed on seasons and regions. Sustain · Separation · Coordination · Beat · Sequence

  • Sensor games

    Control games by holding and moving a new grip that contains a motion sensor. Control · Coordination

  • Finger squeeze game

    Squeeze and release with your fingers while playing a game. Sustain · Control

03Next generation · v3

From fingers to the movement of the hand

We plan to develop a next-generation grip (v3) with a motion sensor (such as a gyro) that reads the orientation (tilt and rotation), position and movement path of the hand together with finger input.

  • A sensor that reads orientation and position

    A motion sensor inside the grip reads the tilt and rotation of the hand, and where it moved from and to (position and path).

  • Hand-movement contents

    We are building new contents in which you move your hand while holding the grip. Finger input and hand movement are used together.

  • Plan suggestions from records

    We are planning a feature that gathers finger and movement records and suggests a training plan. Staff decide whether to apply it.

The next generation (v3) is a development plan, and its items and schedule may change. Any values it keeps would also be records of how training was done, not a diagnosis or a judgment of effect. The video above is a concept illustration, not an actual product or app screen.
