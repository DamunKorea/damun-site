A haptic grip where each finger presses on its own

Following guidance from the screen, sound and vibration, users move their fingers and take part in tasks that involve remembering or reacting to a beat. We aim to combine a variety of activities with performance records so that repeated exercise stays engaging.

  • Independent levers and vibration

    Each of the four levers on a hand presses on its own, and vibration tells you which finger.

  • Sight, hearing and touch together

    Guidance on the screen, in sound and by vibration carries the same signal.

  • A left-and-right pair

    A pair of left and right grips connects to the app as numbers 1 to 8.

The structure of independent input and vibration is the subject of three domestic patent applications (pending, not yet granted).

More on measurement and technology

Press, match, remember

Press the eight buttons on the grips as the screen tells you.

Press the number lit in blue and keep holding for the set time. Starting with one finger and working up to all four fingers of both hands, in a variety of shapes.

A circle falls from above in time with the music; press that number when it fills the ring. Play at a slower or faster tempo, and as you do well, instruments are added to the song one by one.

Numbers appear one at a time. Remember them in order and press them in the same order. It's okay to get one wrong: we show the correct answer and move on to the next number.

All contents

See the contents Rehaptic offers, together with the areas each one covers.

  • Hand exercise

    Press the finger of the lit number and keep holding for a set time. Sustain · Separation · Coordination

  • Rhythm play

    Press falling circles in time with the background music. Beat · Coordination · Sustain

  • Follow the Numbers

    Watch numbers, remember them and press them in the same order. Sequence · Separation

  • Mole catching

    A reaction game in which you press the lit finger to catch the mole that pops up. Reaction · Separation

  • Fist Dodge

    Squeeze the whole left hand to steer the hot-air balloon left and the whole right hand to steer it right, dodging the dark clouds. Coordination · Sustain

  • One finger at a time

    Raise only the target bar out of four and keep the others still. Separation · Control

  • Depth matching

    Match the height of the band on the screen bar and stay at the set depth. Control · Sustain

  • Follow the beat

    An activity that goes deeper into the beat. Beat

  • Seasonal and regional theme packs

    Content bundles themed on seasons and regions. Sustain · Separation · Coordination · Beat · Sequence

  • Sensor games

    Control games by holding and moving a new grip that contains a gyro sensor. Control · Coordination

  • Finger grip-strength exercise

    Enjoy games while also exercising the grip of your fingers. Sustain · Control

Next generation · v3

From fingers to the movement of the hand

The next-generation grip (v3) carries a motion sensor (such as a gyro) and reads the orientation (tilt and rotation), position and movement path of the hand together with finger input. It leads to new contents and AI analysis.

  • A sensor that reads orientation and position

    A motion sensor inside the grip reads the tilt and rotation of the hand, and where it moved from and to (position and path).

  • Hand-movement contents

    We are building new contents in which you move your hand while holding the grip. Finger input and hand movement are used together.

  • AI analysis of measured data

    Finger records and movement records are gathered together to summarize trends and suggest plans. Staff confirm before applying.

The next generation (v3) is a development plan, and its items and schedule may change. Measured values are records of how training was done, not a diagnosis or a judgment of effect. The pictures and video above are concept illustrations, not actual product or app screens.
